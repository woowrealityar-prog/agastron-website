About Agastron
 The holding company behind RulerInsights, Aranidhi, NeoFairs and Mayvrion.
Who We Are
 Agastron Group LLC is a unified holding enterprise that owns and governs four high-growth subsidiary businesses spanning AI-driven IT services, financial intelligence, governance intelligence, and MSME global trade. Founded to build institutions of scale, Agastron provides centralised capital, strategic direction, compliance, and brand governance to its subsidiaries while protecting the group's intellectual property and ensuring structural integrity across verticals.
Our Vision
 To be Asia's most trusted multi-vertical technology holding company — building institutions, not just businesses.
Our Mission
Hold majority stakes and governing authority across every subsidiary.
Raise capital at group level and allocate it efficiently across every vertical.
Protect core IP, platforms and brand assets at the holding level, with risk isolated through a multi-entity structure.
